## Brand & Style

The design system embodies a serene, editorial, and celebratory spirit tailored for welcoming a new life. Grounded in timeless warmth, the aesthetic eschews juvenile clichés in favor of poetic elegance, mindful space, and tactile restraint. It celebrates David's arrival with a gentle balance of quiet luxury and organic warmth.

The visual direction merges **Refined Editorial Minimalism** with **Organic Tactility**:
- Generous cream margins evoke fine-milled stationery and archival cardstock.
- Nature-inspired sage and eucalyptus tones cultivate a protective, soothing sanctuary.
- Understated champagne and brushed gold touches add celebratory luminescence without gaudiness.
- High typographic contrast pairs literary, romantic serif headlines with crisp, functional modern grotesk details.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from botanical specimen papers, sunlit stone, and heirloom linen:

- **Primary (`#5A7263` — Deep Eucalyptus)**: A grounded, quiet sage-pine that provides reliable contrast for key actions, primary links, and essential structural anchors.
- **Secondary (`#8A9A86` — Dusty Herb)**: A tender, muted leaf tone used for supporting badging, secondary buttons, subtle illustrations, and tonal backgrounds.
- **Tertiary (`#C5A880` — Warm Champagne / Brushed Gold)**: A festive, delicate metallic warmth reserved for celebratory accents, monograms, event dates, and focused highlights.
- **Neutral (`#F8F6F0` — Warm Linen / Ecru)**: The primary canvas tone, offering a tactile paper-like warmth far softer and more welcoming than stark digital white.

### Functional Roles
- **Surface Canvas**: `#F8F6F0`
- **Card / Elevation Surface**: `#FFFFFF`
- **Subtle Surface Container**: `#F0EDE4`
- **Text / Headline Primary**: `#232B25` (Deep Forest Charcoal)
- **Text Muted / Body Secondary**: `#545F56` (Warm Olive Slate)
- **Border / Hairline Dividers**: `#E2DDD2`

## Typography

The type system creates an inviting cadence between classical, editorial warmth and effortless modern legibility:

- **Headlines & Titles (`EB Garamond`)**: Conveys timeless grace and gentle heritage. Used for hero invitations, David's name, formal welcoming statements, and registry header titles. Italicize select honorific phrases or dates to induce a poetic, bespoke touch.
- **Body & Metadata (`Manrope`)**: Provides pristine clarity across addresses, RSVP forms, schedules, and registry gift details. Its open geometric forms counterbalance the decorative serif.
- **Labels & Micro-copy**: Employs uppercase tracking (`0.06em` to `0.08em`) in `Manrope` to emulate high-end architectural stationery notes and timeline timestamps.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a centered, card-and-column layout calibrated for quiet breathing room and focused narrative sequencing.

### Grid Structure
- **Desktop (>= 1024px)**: Single centered container constrained to a maximum width of `720px` for singular invitation views, or a 12-column grid (`max-width: 1140px`, `24px` gutter, `48px` margins) for expanded registry and itinerary dashboards.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8-column layout with `24px` gutters and `32px` page margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout with `16px` gutters and `20px` margins. Layouts reflow vertically into single-column editorial cards with relaxed spacing (`space-xl` between sections).

### Spacing Philosophy
Whitespace is treated as an active design material. Large vertical pauses (`space-3xl` to `space-4xl`) separate the invitation header, the event details, the location map, and the gift registry to keep users from feeling rushed.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated through **tonal layering** and **whisper-soft ambient diffusion**, mimicking physical stationery placed upon warm fabric surfaces:

- **Level 0 (Canvas)**: Base linen background (`#F8F6F0`).
- **Level 1 (Stationery Sheet / Card)**: Pure paper white (`#FFFFFF`) with a hair-thin border (`1px solid #E2DDD2`) and an ambient shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(90, 114, 99, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.02)`. The sage tint in the ambient blur keeps the atmosphere gentle and cohesive.
- **Level 2 (Popovers & Modals - RSVP & Registry Overlays)**: Elevated white cards featuring `0 16px 40px -4px rgba(35, 43, 37, 0.08), 0 6px 12px -2px rgba(35, 43, 37, 0.03)`.
- **Accents**: Foil and metallic elements do not use harsh outer glows; they use a fine `1px` inner highlight or soft borders in `#C5A880`.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a **soft, bespoke architectural rounding** (`roundedness: 1`). Corners maintain the dignified crispness of artisanal paper cards while softening digital interactions:

- **Base Radius**: `0.25rem` (4px) for subtle chips, inputs, and minor UI controls.
- **Card & Container Radius**: `0.5rem` (8px) to `0.75rem` (12px) for invitations, photo frames, and itinerary panels.
- **Pill Exceptions**: Interactive tags and secondary RSVP pill badges can selectively use full rounding (`9999px`) when acting as self-contained status chips.
